peakwhere

Where do your peaks land?

Drop in a gene annotation and your ChIP-seq, CUT&RUN or ATAC-seq peaks, and see how they split between promoters, UTRs, exons, introns and intergenic space, one bar per file.

Your files never leave your computer.

Everything runs here, in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, there is no server and no account, and the page makes no requests to anywhere else: no analytics, no fonts, no CDNs.

1 Gene annotation

One GFF3 or GTF file, plain or .gz. GFF3 is preferred: it has UTRs split by type and chromosome lengths.

or drop it here
Optional: chromosome sizes

A chrom.sizes file (two columns: name, length) lets the chart show a Genome background bar when the annotation doesn't give chromosome lengths, as a GTF never does.

or drop it here

2 Peak files

BED, narrowPeak or broadPeak, plain or .gz; or CSV/TSV with chr, start and end columns. Each file becomes one bar, in the order added.

Drop peak files here
